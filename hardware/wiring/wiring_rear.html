Drone synth panel → Daisy Seed3 wiring — VIEWED FROM BEHIND THE PANEL

Mirror image of the front. Knob 1 is at the top-right, knob 12 at the bottom-left. ONE RULE FOR EVERY POT, seen from behind: LEFT terminal = VCC, MIDDLE = output, RIGHT = ground. Rows mounted upside down turn the opposite way; the firmware invert table corrects that.

FIRST WIRE, BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: Seed pin 20 (AGND) to Seed pin 40 (DGND). The Seed3 does not bond them. Without this jumper every knob reads full scale.

Every pot is wired the same way. A row that turns the wrong way is fixed in kPotInvert in synth_main.cpp, never by rewiring. All V terminals join one red bus per group, all G terminals one black bus per group. Each group sends one red lead to pin 21 and one black lead to pin 20. Pin 20 is jumpered to pin 40. Pins 33 and 34 are skipped on purpose (no ADC there).
