Drone synth panel → Daisy Seed3 wiring

FIRST WIRE, BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: Seed pin 20 (AGND) to Seed pin 40 (DGND). The Seed3 does not bond them. Without this jumper every knob reads full scale.

All V terminals join one red bus per group, all G terminals one black bus per group. Each group sends one red lead to pin 21 and one black lead to pin 20. Pin 20 is jumpered to pin 40. Pins 33 and 34 are skipped on purpose (no ADC there).
